# HealthTrackk Privacy Policy Template

Use this as a starting point for your hosted privacy policy page before Play Store submission.

## Privacy Policy

HealthTrackk collects and processes limited health-related and account-related information to provide screening tools, dashboard features, chatbot assistance, vaccination tracking, and emergency support features.

### Information collected

- Name
- Email address
- Login credentials
- Health profile information entered by the user
- Symptom assessment responses
- Prediction history
- Vaccination records
- Chatbot messages
- Approximate or precise location when the user requests nearby hospital search

### How information is used

- To create and manage user accounts
- To provide health dashboards and user-entered records
- To power symptom assessment and AI assistant features
- To show nearby hospitals when location access is granted
- To improve reliability, safety, and support

### Location data

Location is accessed only when the user chooses features that require it, such as nearby hospital search or emergency location assistance.

### Data sharing

HealthTrackk does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by service providers used for hosting, analytics, email delivery, mapping, or AI functionality, only as needed to operate the service.

### Data retention

User data is retained while the account is active or as required for legal, security, and operational purposes.

### User rights

Users may request access, correction, or deletion of their account data, subject to applicable law.

### Security

HealthTrackk uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.
